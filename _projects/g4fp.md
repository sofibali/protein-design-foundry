Every other project here lands on the same two readouts, time-resolved FRET and
luminescence. A facility that only ever runs two assay formats will quietly
optimise itself around them, in scheduling, in reagent stocking and in which
plate types get validated.

This project exists to stop that. It is early and deliberately thin: the useful
output at this stage is a validated fluorescence workflow on the reader and an
honest account of what the format costs in dispenser and reader time compared
with the established routes.
