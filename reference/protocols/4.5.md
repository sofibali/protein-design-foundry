# STAR Methods — 4.5 Magnetic bead purification

_Working document, 2026-11-23. Derived from `_data/protocols.yml`
(`workflows:`, id `4.5`) and from the citation audit in
`00-citation-audit.md`. Intended practice; the facility installs April 2027._

**Citation status: vendor method over a published affinity-tag system.**
The bind/wash/elute cycle as written is the bead manufacturer's. The tag
system is published: Schmidt & Skerra, *Nat Protoc* 2007,
[10.1038/nprot.2007.209](https://doi.org/10.1038/nprot.2007.209), and for
the Twin-Strep tag the constructs actually carry, Schmidt et al., *Protein
Expr Purif* 2013,
[10.1016/j.pep.2013.08.021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pep.2013.08.021).
Both DOIs resolved live 2026-11-23; neither is cited in the repository.

## Resource availability

**Lead contact.** As 4.1.

**Materials availability.** Output is **micrograms** of eluted protein per
well, in the bead chemistry's elution buffer. Crystallography protein does
not come from this protocol, and the gap to the ≥10 mg/mL that 4.11 needs is
not close.

**Data and code availability.** No analysis code specific to this protocol.

## Experimental model and subject details

Not applicable. The input is lysate or a cell-free reaction; no living
material is handled.

## Method details

**Scale.** 96 per plate, about one hour.

1. **Bind.** Beads added by the 96-channel head and resuspended at
   200–3,000 rpm on the deck shaker, right beside the magnet. ~8 min at 96.
2. **Wash.** The plate never leaves the deck between washes. That is the only
   reason the protocol fits in an hour.
3. **Elute** into the bead chemistry's buffer.
4. **Hold or hand on.** 4 °C random-access storage, or straight into 4.6.

**Bead separation is the Bravo's job, not the Magniflex's.** Despite the
name, a Cavro Magni Flex is a 2/4/8-channel pipetting framework with piston
pumps and no magnet.

**This is the most expensive hour on the system.** At 96 lysates per hour it
is the dominant cost in the *E. coli* route — a full 14-plate round is a 14 h
serial step. The repository records that heat lysis at 95 °C in a 96-well
ATC block removes it entirely, 14 h to 2.1 h, at no hardware cost.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

No quantification happens here. Recovery is assumed rather than measured in
this protocol; the concentration of what came off is first measured in 4.6,
and `protocols/express.cfps.doe.md` carries the working assumption of **50%
bead recovery** when converting the ~2.25 µg of purified protein a full
assay ladder consumes per design into the ~4.5 µg of crude needed upstream.
That 50% is a planning figure, not a measurement, and should be measured on
the first runs.

**Gate D2** (is there enough, and is it clean enough?) is evaluated on the
output of this protocol, by 4.6.
