# STAR Methods — 4.9 Dose-response

_Working document, 2026-11-23. Derived from `_data/protocols.yml`
(`workflows:`, id `4.9`) and from the citation audit in
`00-citation-audit.md`. Intended practice; the facility installs April 2027._

**Citation status: standard practice.** A dilution series, a fit, and a K_D
or IC50 per hit. What is distinctive is building the series in the
destination plate from a single titrant source rather than serially — a
property of acoustic transfer (Ellson et al., *JALA* 2003,
[10.1016/s1535-5535(03)00011-x](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1535-5535(03)00011-x),
resolved live 2026-11-23) applied by this deck. That is an implementation
choice, not a published assay method, and nothing here should be cited as
though it were.

## Method details

**Scale.** 384 per plate, 1–2 h. The whole series for a set of hits fits on
one plate.

1. **Build the dilution series** acoustically in the destination plate from a
   single titrant source. ~3 min per plate. A serial dilution on the
   96-channel head would instead cost ~32 min of the system's bottleneck
   instrument, which is the reason this step is on the Echo.
2. **Backfill and develop.** Bulk dispense does both in one pass.
3. **Seal and equilibrate.**
4. **Read the series** — one plate, ~8 min for TR-FRET.
5. **Fit**, off the deck.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

A **K_D or IC50 per hit**, from a fitted concentration–response curve, with
the fit retained behind the number. No fit model, point count, replicate
count or goodness-of-fit threshold is stated in `_data/protocols.yml` for
this protocol.

For scale, the per-design mass budget in `protocols/express.cfps.doe.md`
assumes a **12-point, 3-fold series topping out at 1 µM, 20 µL per well, in
triplicate** — 1.26 µg of purified protein per design, which is more than
half the ~2.25 µg the whole assay ladder consumes. That makes this the most
protein-hungry assay in the set, and it is the reason D5 sits behind a
cheaper single-point gate rather than being run on everything.

**Gate D5** is decided here. Criteria live in `_data/gates.yml`.
