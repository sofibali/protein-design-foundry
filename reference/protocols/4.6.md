## Method details

**Scale.** 384 per plate, 30 min. Three reads of one plate plus a
normalisation pass.

1. **Concentration.** Split-GFP or HiBiT reagent from the bulk dispenser,
   read against a standard curve carried on the same plate. The bench
   alternative, A280 on a spectrophotometer, gives a reading that depends on
   how many tryptophans the design happens to have, which is exactly what
   makes designs non-comparable.
2. **Nucleic-acid carryover.** An aliquot into a UV-transparent plate,
   A260/A280, read in the same pass.
3. **Aggregation.** FP tracer added, polarisation read. A rise against the
   monomer control is the flag. **It detects aggregation, not a change of
   oligomer state** — the distinction matters and the repository is already
   careful about it.
4. **Normalise the plate before any assay sees it.** The Lynx's in-line flow
   sensor on every channel measures what was actually delivered, so the
   plate is normalised **by measurement rather than by nominal volume**.
   1 µL minimum, 2 µL at 2% CV. This is what lets 4.9's concentration series
   start from a flat plate instead of from whatever each well expressed.
5. **Band identity.** Still a gel, on a subset, off the deck. Purity in that
   sense is the one question this protocol cannot answer, and the repository
   says so rather than implying otherwise.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

- **Concentration**: RFU or luminescence converted against the on-plate
  standard curve. No fit-quality threshold is stated for this protocol; 4.3
  uses R² < 0.98 as its rejection criterion for the equivalent curve and the
  same threshold would be the consistent choice.
- **Carryover**: A260/A280 ratio per well. No threshold is stated in the
  repository.
- **Aggregation**: polarisation per well relative to the monomer control.
  Reported as a flag, not as a quantity — there is no oligomer-state
  measurement behind it.

**Gate D2** is decided on this protocol's output. The mass floor it is
checked against (~2 µg of purified protein per design, stated as a mass
rather than a concentration so it does not depend on the production reaction
volume) is derived in `protocols/express.cfps.doe.md`.
