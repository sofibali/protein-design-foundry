# STAR Methods — 4.4 *E. coli* expression, arrayed

_Working document, 2026-11-23. Derived from `_data/protocols.yml`
(`workflows:`, id `4.4`) and from the citation audit in
`00-citation-audit.md`. Intended practice; the facility installs April 2027._

**Citation status: standard practice with one canonical published
component.** Autoinduction in ZYM-5052 is Studier, *Protein Expr Purif*
2005, [10.1016/j.pep.2005.01.016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pep.2005.01.016)
— "Protein production by auto-induction in high-density shaking cultures"
(DOI resolved live 2026-11-23). The repository names the medium and not the
paper; this is the clearest case in the set of a published method used by
name without attribution. Everything else — deep-well block culture, a
gas-permeable seal, plate centrifugation, detergent lysis — is ordinary
arrayed-expression practice with no single source.

## Resource availability

**Lead contact.** As 4.1.

**Materials availability.** The output is a deep-well block of cleared
lysate, one culture per well, handed to 4.5. No clone is isolated and no
glycerol stock is made: nothing in this protocol or in 4.14 returns a colony
from agar back to liquid.

**Data and code availability.** No analysis code is specific to this
protocol.

## Experimental model and subject details

*E. coli* BL21(DE3), grown in arrayed liquid culture in a 96-well deep-well
block, one culture per well, overnight. Growth is on the Cytomat, which
shakes and incubates — not on the Bravo deck nests, which hold a fixed
temperature and do not aerate.

**Open hardware question recorded in the repository:** whether the Cytomat's
temperature range covers 30 °C. If it does, yeast and slow-folding
constructs stop being blocked on hardware. `reference/capabilities.md`
records this as resolved in the C-LiN series' favour (it runs to 50 °C),
with the orbit rather than the temperature as the remaining issue, so the
`blocked:` line on the workflow may be stale.

## Method details

**Scale.** 96 per plate, overnight.

1. **Inoculate into autoinduction medium.** Medium bulk-dispensed into the
   deep-well block, then inoculated from the transformation (4.14).
2. **Apply the gas-permeable seal** on the second sealer.
3. **Grow overnight** in a shaking, incubating Cytomat position. This, not
   the liquid handler, is the throughput cap on everything that grows.
4. **Harvest the cells.** Two plates per run at 5000 g, about 20 s to speed,
   scheduled against everything else in flight.
5. **Lyse and clear.** Detergent lysis from the bulk dispenser, then a second
   spin.

Grown blocks come off the Cytomat into the 4 °C SteriStore; static,
non-shaking recovery goes to the 37 °C one.

**Two cautions from Studier 2005 that the repository does not carry**, and
that matter for an unattended overnight on a Kan backbone: kanamycin at
100 µg/mL rather than 30 in high-phosphate media, and high aeration
inhibiting induction at low lactose.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

None in this protocol. It produces material, not a measurement; yield per
well is first quantified in 4.6, after 4.5 has purified it. Gate D1 (does it
express?) is answered here only in the sense that a well either yields
usable lysate or does not — the quantitative form of D1 is run in 4.3.
