## Resource availability

**Lead contact.** As 4.1.

**Materials availability.** One circular, expression-ready construct per
design, in plate format. No colonies and no glycerol stocks exist, so there
is nothing to re-grow: a construct that is used up is re-assembled from the
fragment plate (4.1) rather than retrieved from a strain collection.

**Data and code availability.** No analysis code is specific to this
protocol. The per-design identity rests on the ordered fragment and is not
confirmed by sequencing anywhere in this route — see the limitation below.

## Experimental model and subject details

**Not applicable, deliberately.** There is no transformation, no outgrowth,
no colony and no miniprep. The whole point of the protocol is that Golden
Gate closes a circle *in vitro*, so no host is involved at any stage. This
is the step the deck could not do well, and it turns out not to be needed.

## Method details

**Scale.** 96–384 per run, one day.

1. **Retrieval PCR off the fragment plate.** Built acoustically in the 384
   block; one of four cyclers runs it. ~3 min of Echo time per plate.
2. **Clean up the insert.** SPRI at 1.8× on the deck magnet, eluted small.
   ~32 min at 384.
3. **Normalise insert against vector by mass.** Acoustic transfer at a fixed
   mass, so the insert-to-vector molar ratio is held. **This is the one
   deliberate departure from the source paper**, which added a fixed volume
   and let the ratio drift. The ratio is the single variable this step
   exists to control.
4. **Close the circle by Golden Gate.** Positive-displacement delivery of the
   glycerol-based enzyme mix, then 30 × (37 °C 60 s / 16 °C 60 s) and 60 °C
   for 10 min in a 96 block.
5. **Go straight into the expression reaction.** A second SPRI, then the
   circular product is dispensed into the cell-free reaction.

**What the format costs.** The two SPRI cleanups are the only Bravo steps
here, so taking the assembly from 96 to 384 quadruples them — 16 min becomes
64. That is the trade to weigh before scaling this protocol up, and it is
the reason the `cost:` entry names the Bravo as the binding instrument.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

No statistical analysis. Two quantitative controls carry the protocol: the
mass-normalised insert:vector ratio at step 3, and the small elution volume
at step 2 that keeps the assembly concentrated enough for a 30 µL reaction.

**Stated limitation.** Because nothing is cloned, nothing is sequenced. The
source paper's own pipeline has the same property, and the CF2H work found
that unsequenced DNA is the main false-negative source in a cell-free
screen — a design that fails once is a candidate for re-synthesis rather
than a dead design. That caveat belongs to any route built on 4.13.
