# STAR Methods — 4.12 Compound and fragment screening

_Working document, 2026-11-23. Derived from `_data/protocols.yml`
(`workflows:`, id `4.12`) and from the citation audit in
`00-citation-audit.md`. Intended practice; the facility installs April 2027._

**Citation status: this facility's own, built entirely on the 4.7 spine.**
There is no separate method to cite: the assay is 4.7's, the transfer is
acoustic DMSO transfer (Ellson et al., *JALA* 2003,
[10.1016/s1535-5535(03)00011-x](https://doi.org/10.1016/s1535-5535(03)00011-x)),
and percent effect against the plate's own controls is ordinary screening
practice. Yin et al., *Acta Crystallogr D* 2014,
[10.1107/S1399004713034603](https://doi.org/10.1107/S1399004713034603), is
the relevant precedent **only** if this is ever run as acoustic *in situ*
co-crystallisation rather than as a plate assay. Both resolved live
2026-11-23.

## Method details

**Scale.** 1536, 1–2 h. One 1536 plate at the same plate cost as 4.7 for
four times the wells.

1. **Transfer compound from the DMSO library**, acoustically and tipless at
   1536. The bench alternative is a pin tool or a multichannel, with the
   carryover a pin tool brings.
2. **Add protein and target on top** — the same assay spine as 4.7, built
   acoustically over the compound.
3. **Develop, seal and equilibrate** as 4.7, from a different source plate.
4. **Read.** One 1536 plate instead of four 384s.
5. **Report, and put the library back.** The compound source plate returns to
   ambient storage.

**Why this protocol exists.** It is essentially free once 4.7 works, and it
is what makes the foundry useful to labs that are not designing proteins at
all.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

**Percent effect per compound, against the plate's own controls.** No hit
threshold, Z′ requirement, replicate count or counter-screen for assay
interference is stated in `_data/protocols.yml`.

Two things a compound screen normally needs that the repository does not yet
specify, and that are recorded here as gaps rather than filled in:

- A **plate quality statistic** (Z′ or equivalent) computed from the on-plate
  controls, without which percent effect at 1536 has no error bar.
- A **DMSO tolerance check** for the chosen assay format, since the compound
  arrives in DMSO and the four formats in 4.7 do not tolerate it equally.

The workflow carries no gate.
