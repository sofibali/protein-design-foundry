## Resource availability

**Lead contact.** As 4.1.

**Materials availability.** The output is expressed protein in a sealed
plate, consumed by 4.5 (purification) or read directly by a crude-tolerant
binding format. The positive-control construct is the one item that must
persist across every round of every target, indefinitely, for drift to stay
visible.

**Data and code availability.** The design matrix, well assignments and
randomisation seed (**20270401**) are recorded in
`protocols/express.cfps.doe.md`. The seed must not be re-rolled between
rounds or plates stop being comparable.

## Experimental model and subject details

No living organism. The expression system is an *E. coli*-derived lysate or
a reconstituted PURE system; "lot" is treated as an experimental factor
because extract lots differ enough to move the answer.

## Method details

**Design.** Six factors (Mg-glutamate, K-glutamate, energy, reaction volume,
template form, extract lot), 16 conditions, 2^(6−2) resolution IV, four
replicates per condition, one target per round.

**Temperature is deliberately outside the matrix.** It cannot be randomised
within a plate, so including it would make this a split-plot design and the
analysis would be wrong. Instead the same plate runs in all four ATCs at
four temperatures — suggested arms 20 / 25 / 30 / 37 °C — which makes
temperature a real replicated factor across plates.

**On deck.**

1. **Build the 16 conditions and add template.** Stocks dispensed acoustically
   straight into the destination, template on top. ~3 min per plate.
2. **Dispense the common lysate or PURE mix** to final volume. A whole 1536
   plate at 1 µL per well takes about 21 s.
3. **Seal** with thermal foil.
4. **Incubate 4–6 h**, one temperature per block, four blocks running
   independent programs at once. The ATC is used here as a sealed,
   lid-heated, precisely held incubator rather than as a cycler — which also
   controls the evaporation that otherwise ruins edge wells.
5. **Peel and develop.** Up to 200 seals an hour off the peeler, then GFP1-10
   in excess, 30 min to complement.
6. **Read** against the on-plate standard curve, ~8 min per plate.

**Controls, same plate.** 8 no-template wells (per-condition background),
8 positive-control wells, 16 standard-curve wells.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

1. Subtract per-condition no-template background.
2. Convert RFU to µg/mL against the on-plate standard curve; **reject the
   fit if R² < 0.98**.
3. Fit main effects plus estimable two-factor interactions, with temperature
   arm as a factor across the four plates.
4. Report effect size and confidence interval per factor. A factor whose CI
   crosses zero is not carried into round 2.
5. Rank conditions by mean yield; report the best and its replicate SD.

**Gate D1 — does it express?** Pass is at least one condition at ≥5 µg/mL in
≥3 of 4 replicates. Fail is no condition reaching 5 µg/mL at any
temperature. Ambiguous is exactly 2 of 4 replicates, or only at one edge
temperature. The 5 µg/mL floor is set from what the assay ladder needs
downstream, not from what the expression system can do, and the derivation
is recorded in `protocols/express.cfps.doe.md`.
