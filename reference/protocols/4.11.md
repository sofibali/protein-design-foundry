# STAR Methods — 4.11 Crystallisation screening

_Working document, 2026-11-23. Derived from `_data/protocols.yml`
(`workflows:`, id `4.11`) and from the citation audit in
`00-citation-audit.md`. Intended practice; the facility installs April 2027.
The repository marks this protocol **offline** — it needs an Echo 6XX, and
imaging is unquoted._

**Citation status: two published methods, neither cited in the
repository.**

- Commercial sparse-matrix screens, which are the entire premise of
  "condition matrices": Jancarik & Kim, *J Appl Crystallogr* 1991,
  [10.1107/S0021889891004430](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0021889891004430).
- Acoustic drop setting and acoustic *in situ* co-crystallisation against
  fragment libraries: Yin et al., *Acta Crystallogr D* 2014,
  [10.1107/S1399004713034603](https://doi.org/10.1107/S1399004713034603).

Both resolved live 2026-11-23. The Yin paper is the closest published
precedent for what 4.11 and 4.12 propose together, and is the one citation
in this audit that would most change what a reader thinks the protocol is.

## Resource availability

**Lead contact.** As 4.1.

**Materials availability.** The protein does not come from this facility's
automated route. Anything run here depends on an offline flask-and-column
preparation reaching milligram scale at ≥10 mg/mL, which is a precondition
rather than a step.

**Data and code availability.** Scoring is by eye at a microscope and
produces clear / precipitate / crystal calls. No scoring code exists,
because no imager is quoted.

Not applicable.

## Method details

**Scale.** 1536 drops. Hours to set, weeks to grow.

1. **Make the protein.** Off the deck: flask expression and column
   purification to milligram scale at ≥10 mg/mL.
2. **Set the drops.** Acoustic sitting drops at 1536, protein against
   condition, apo or co-crystallised. Eighty trays is about 11.5 h
   on-system.
3. **Close and store the trays.** A lid, never a seal. Trays sit on the
   AmbiStore carousel.
4. **Image and score.** Still by hand, at a microscope. Eighty trays is about
   48.9 h of hand scoring — against 11.5 h to set them, which is the whole
   argument for quoting an imager.

**Stated blocker.** Needs the Echo 6XX rather than the 525: the 525 cannot
do DMSO, 1536, or the drop volumes this needs. Imagers and automated scoring
are unquoted.

## Quantification and statistical analysis

Drops are **scored categorically — clear, precipitate or crystal** — not
measured. No hit rate, scoring rubric or inter-scorer agreement procedure is
stated in `_data/protocols.yml`. With scoring done by eye and by hand, a
written rubric is the obvious thing this protocol will need before it runs
at 1536.

**Gate D7** is attached to this workflow; its criteria live in
`_data/gates.yml`. The protocol ends at imaging, then diffraction, both off
the deck.
