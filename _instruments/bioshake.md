It matters for one workflow more than any other. Magnetic bead purification
is the most expensive hour on the system, and every wash needs the beads
resuspended. Doing that on the deck, beside the magnet, is what keeps the
plate from making a round trip to another cart between each of three washes.

It is also the mixing step after SOC addition in
[heat-shock transformation]({{ '/inventory/bravo/' | relative_url }}), where
the recovery culture has to be homogeneous before it goes to the Cytomat.
